Method & caveats. Canonical unit is kt/yr of total renewable product (HVO/RD + SAF + bio-naphtha/LPG where applicable), converted from quoted local units (≈2.95 kt per million US gal; ≈45 kt per 1,000 b/d). "Max SAF" is maximum SAF capability within total capacity, not dedicated output — most HEFA units flex RD↔SAF. Entries flagged est are estimated where operators do not publish a figure (most co-processing units, several Chinese plants). Chinese plant-level data is the least reliable; export "white-list" quotas (1.2 Mt/yr across Jiaao, Haike, EcoCeres, Sanju as of late 2025) are noted where they proxy capacity. Announced projects are shown at face value — historical attrition on announced HVO/SAF projects is high. Co-processing renewable content at conventional refineries is inherently approximate. This is a snapshot, not a live feed — ask me to refresh any region.
